1About the Application

Meal Tracker is a mobile-first web application that runs an office mess: who eats which meal on which day, what the mess spends, what each person owes at the end of the month, and how many plates the cook has to prepare today.

1.1Why it exists

Before an app, an office mess runs on a paper register. Three problems repeat every month:

  • Nobody knows today's plate count until it is too late. The cook guesses, and food is wasted or runs short.
  • The register is edited after the fact. Somebody "remembers" they skipped dinner three days ago and the totals stop being trustworthy.
  • Month-end billing is a night of arithmetic. Meals × rate, minus deposits, per person, by hand.

Meal Tracker fixes all three: employees mark their own meals in advance, meals lock themselves at a published deadline, and every bill is calculated by the database from the records that already exist — so the numbers can never go stale.

1.4How the money works

Four formulas run the whole application. Everything else on screen is one of these four applied to a different slice of data.

daily meal count   = breakfast ON × breakfast count + lunch ON × lunch count + dinner ON × dinner count
monthly meal count = sum of the daily meal counts over the manager period's own meals
total bill         = monthly meal count × meal rate
balance            = total deposit − total bill   (> 0 refund · = 0 settled · < 0 due)

A meal count is not the same as a meal. Breakfast is smaller than lunch, so each meal carries a weight. The default weights are Breakfast 0.75 · Lunch 1.25 · Dinner 1.00, and the mess manager can change them for any single date (a biryani night can be worth 2.00). Weights are stored once per date, not per person, so changing a date's lunch count instantly re-prices everyone who had lunch that day.

Guests and mess spending are kept completely separate from employee bills:

guest bill (one date) = breakfast guests × ৳60 + lunch guests × ৳150 + dinner guests × ৳150
total bill to collect = the same, added up over every date of the mess month (paid by the office)
total monthly spending = the sum of every spending entry recorded in the mess month

4.5Who changes the deadlines — and how

The deadlines live on the mess manager's Meal Status tab, in a collapsed strip at the top. Any mess manager can open it; the change applies office-wide and takes effect on every employee's next page load.

4.6What happens after a deadline — exactly

  1. On screen: within 15 seconds the button grows a padlock and stops responding. No reload needed.
  2. In the database: any attempt to write that meal is rejected with MEAL_LOCKED — including an attempt made outside the application, from a script or a modified request.
  3. Your count: the meal immediately joins Meals I had and your total meal count, because it can no longer change.
  4. The cook: the plate count on the meal board is final for that meal.
  5. The fix, if you need one: ask the mess manager. A manager is not bound by the deadlines and can correct any meal of their own running period (Section 8.4).
Warning — the deadline is not a reminder, it is a wall

There is no grace period, no "request a change" button and no appeal inside the application. If you miss the lunch deadline and leave the office, you are billed for that lunch unless a mess manager removes it for you. Set your meals for the whole week in advance — future dates are always open.

  3. Which dates you may set meal counts, guests and spending for.

17Frequently Asked Questions

17.1Meals and deadlines

Why can't I change yesterday's meal?
Previous days are locked for every employee, always, with no exception and no grace period. Meal records have to stop moving at some point or no bill could ever be trusted. A mess manager can still correct a past date, provided it belongs to their own running period.
Why is today's lunch locked when it is only 11:05?
The lunch deadline is 11:00 AM. The lock takes effect at the deadline to the minute, in Bangladesh time, using the server's clock — so a phone set five minutes slow will still show the meal locked.
What exactly happens after a deadline?
Four things: the button gets a padlock within 15 seconds; the database rejects any attempt to change that meal; the meal joins your "Meals I had" count because it can no longer change; and the cook's plate count for that meal is final.
Can I set my meals for next week?
Yes, as far ahead as you like. Future dates are always editable — the deadline only applies once that day becomes today. This is the recommended way to use the app.
Who can change historical data?
Only the mess manager whose own period owns that meal, and only while their period is running (00:00 on its first day to 23:59 on its last, Bangladesh time). After that, nobody — not the next manager, not an administrator through the interface.
The deadline times in this manual do not match my screen.
The deadlines are a setting a mess manager can change at any time. The line under "My meals" in your panel always prints the real ones — trust that.
I tapped a meal and it went back to how it was, outlined in red.
The save failed — almost always the connection. Tap it again. Nothing was written, so there is nothing to undo.

17.2Meal counts and money

Why is breakfast only 0.75 of a meal?
Because breakfast costs the mess less than lunch. Each meal carries a weight — by default Breakfast 0.75, Lunch 1.25, Dinner 1.00 — so people who eat only breakfast are not billed as if they ate a full lunch.
My meal count went up and I did not eat anything extra.
The mess manager raised a date's meal count for a special meal — a biryani night at dinner 2.00, for example. It applies to everyone who had that meal on that date. The note inside "Meals I had" warns that this can happen.
How is my due amount calculated?
meal count × meal rate = total bill, then total deposit − total bill = balance. A negative balance is shown as a Due; a positive one as a Refund. Every step is printed on your "My bill" card.
"Meals I had" says 67.5 but my bill says 74.25. Which is right?
Both. "Meals I had" counts only meals that can no longer change (past meals). Your bill counts every meal that is ON this month, including ones you have booked for days still to come. By the end of the month the two agree.
Where does the meal rate come from?
The month's team divides the mess's total spending (Spend → Sum Spending) by the month's total meal count (Dashboard) and rounds it. The application does not choose it.
Why can't I see the meal rate yet?
Because it has not been published. A manager can test as many rates as they like during the month; none of them reaches employees until Publish meal rate is pressed.
Is the "dummy meal rate" my real bill?
No. It is a private what-if calculator. The number you type never leaves your phone, is stored under your own account, is erased when you sign out, and has no connection to the manager's rate.
I paid more than my bill. What happens?
Your row shows Remaining (refund) with the amount. The mess manager either hands it back or records it as an advance deposit in the next month.

17.3Guests

How does guest meal billing work?
Guests are billed to the office at fixed rates — Breakfast ৳60, Lunch ৳150, Dinner ৳150 — and the month's total appears as "Total bill to collect" on the Guest tab and "Guest bill to collect" on the Dashboard. No employee's meal count or bill changes by a single taka.
Can employees see guest numbers?
No. The Employee Panel never shows guests. The cook's public board shows today's guest counts (never any money) so the right amount of food is prepared.
Twenty more guests turned up. Do I add a second entry?
No — change the number and save again. There is exactly one guest record per date per month, and saving overwrites it.
Why are the lunch and dinner guest boxes greyed out on the 5th?
The 5th is a handover day. The outgoing month declares breakfast guests; the incoming month declares lunch and dinner. Each month declares only its own meals.

17.4Accounts and access

I forgot my password.
There is no reset e-mail. Ask the mess manager to press Reset login beside your name, then sign up again at /employee/signup with the same Token Number and a new password. Your meals and deposits are untouched.
Someone else signed up with my Token Number.
The mess manager presses Reset login, which deletes that login, and you sign up again immediately. Token numbers are not secret — they are printed on the meal board — so whoever signs up first claims one.
My panel says "Account deactivated".
A mess manager has deactivated you. You can still sign in but cannot set meals or see your bill until you are reactivated. Your history is safe.
Can two people use the mess manager account at once?
Yes — that is the design. One account per month, shared by the whole team of about five. There is no per-person manager login.
Can I see another month's data?
Only by signing in as that month's account. Months are sealed from one another in the database, so changing a URL or an ID returns nothing.

17.5Months and history

What happens at the beginning of a new month?
On the 5th the incoming team takes over from lunch. Nothing is copied forward: every employee starts with all meals OFF, the new month has no rate, no deposits, no guests and no spending. The employee list and the meal deadlines carry over, because they are office-wide.
What happens to the previous month's data?
Nothing — it stays exactly as it was, and the previous team keeps full access to it forever. The only thing they lose is the ability to change employees' meal ON/OFF.
Why does 4 October appear in September's report?
Because a mess month runs 5th to 5th. September 2026 means 5 September lunch through 5 October breakfast, so 1–4 October belong to September.
Why is my dashboard empty at the start of a month?
Because nothing has been recorded yet. Zeros and dashes are the normal empty state. A genuine failure shows a red box with a Try again button instead.

17.6Data and corrections

What should I do if I see incorrect information?
Employees: tell the mess manager, naming the date and the meal — tapping a counter in "Meals I had" gives you the exact dates to quote. Managers: correct it on Meal Status for that date (meals), or Expense Status (deposits — remove the wrong one and add a correct one).
Can a deposit be edited?
No — deposits are removed and re-added. Remove asks for confirmation naming the amount and the employee.
Why won't the app let me delete an employee?
Because they have meals or deposits on record, and billing history must never disappear. Deactivate them instead — they vanish from the meal board but stay in every month's report where they ate or paid.
What does "Not yours" mean on a meal column?
You are looking at a handover day (the 5th) and that meal belongs to the neighbouring month's manager. It is read-only for you and is left out of your meal counts.
"This date is outside your mess month" — what now?
Press Jump to today, or pick a date between your period's first and last dates (shown in the sidebar under your account name).
Is the CSV the same as what I see on screen?
Yes — the same rows and the same numbers, plus the breakfast/lunch/dinner breakdown. It always exports every employee, even when the table on screen is filtered to one.

A.5How this manual was produced

  1. The application was run against an isolated, empty database — the production data was never touched, read or written.
  2. Four manager accounts and fourteen employees were created through the application's own sign-up and Employees screens.
  3. A complete month of meal records, deposits, guest dates, spending entries, custom meal counts and announced menus was loaded, plus a second, finished month (August) and two future months.
  4. Every screen was then opened in a real browser and captured at 2× resolution — populated first, then empty, then in each locked or handover state.
  5. Numbered call-outs were positioned from the live page geometry, so each marker sits on the element it names.
  6. Every figure quoted in the text was read back from the application or the database and checked against the screenshots.
