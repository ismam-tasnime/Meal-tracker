10Guest Meals

Guests are people who eat at the mess but are not on the employee list — visitors, clients, a contractor's team. The office pays for guest meals, not the employees. Guest counts therefore never touch anyone's meal count or bill; they produce a separate "bill to collect" from the office.

10.1The fixed guest rates

MealPer guestExample

These three rates are fixed in the application and cannot be changed from any screen. They have nothing to do with the employee meal rate, which changes every month.

10.3Declaring guests, step by step

  1. Open the Guest tab and navigate to the date.
  2. Type the number of guests for each meal. Leave a box empty for none.
  3. Watch the bill on the right of each row and the total at the bottom — they update as you type.
  4. Press Save guests. The confirmation reads: "Guests saved for 29 September 2026. The cook sees them on the meal board."
Saving a date again overwrites it

There is exactly one guest record per date per month — never a second one. If ten more guests turn up, change the number and save again; do not try to add a second entry. Clear guests (with confirmation) removes the date's record entirely.

What is different between a date with guests and a date without?
  • The corner label: Saved (or Not saved yet while you are typing) versus No guests.
  • The per-meal amounts and the total: real money versus ৳0.
  • The Clear guests button exists only when something is saved.
  • The date appears in — or is absent from — the "Guests this month" list below. There are never blank rows for empty dates.
What the form refuses

Guests must be whole numbers from 0 to 10,000. Type "2.5" or "abc" and the box turns red, its amount becomes a dash with the hint "Whole number", and Save guests is disabled until it is fixed.

No bill is ever stored. Only the guest counts are saved; every amount on this page — per meal, per date, per month — is recalculated from those counts each time the page is drawn, so it cannot drift out of date.

10.6What the cook sees

Guests are never mixed into the employee count

The big number at the top of each card stays the employee count; guests sit underneath it, clearly labelled, and only the indigo "To prepare" box adds the two together. The cook's board never shows any money. If no guests are declared for today, the amber and indigo blocks do not appear at all and the board looks exactly as it would without the feature.

10.7Guests on the 5th (handover day)

Each month keeps its own guest record for the 5th, so a date + meal still has exactly one guest count — and each month's guest bill contains only its own meals.
