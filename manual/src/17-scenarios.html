16Common User Scenarios

Scenario 1 — "I want lunch tomorrow"

Situation. It is Tuesday evening. Romith will be in the office tomorrow and wants breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Steps.

  1. Open /employee and sign in with the Token Number.
  2. Press Next → once — the date becomes 30 September and the "Today" badge disappears.
  3. Tap any meal that is not already ON. Each tap saves instantly.
  4. There is no banner and no padlock, because future dates are always editable.
Result. Romith is counted in tomorrow's plate count from the moment he taps. He can change his mind any time up to 8:00 AM for breakfast, 11:00 AM for lunch and 5:00 PM for dinner tomorrow.

Scenario 2 — "I forgot to cancel breakfast before the deadline"

Situation. It is 9:30 AM. Breakfast was ON but Romith did not come in. The 8:00 AM deadline has passed.

Steps.

  1. Romith cannot fix this himself — the button has a padlock and the database would refuse the change anyway.
  2. He tells the mess manager, naming the date and the meal.
  3. The manager opens Meal Status, navigates to that date, finds row 101 and taps Breakfast off.
  4. Romith's meal count for that date drops by 0.75 immediately.
Result. Corrected — but only because the manager's period is still running. Ask on the day; after the period ends nobody can change it.

Scenario 3 — "I need to correct someone's meal"

Situation. Two employees tell the manager they were marked wrongly for 29 September.

Steps.

  1. Meal Status → set the date.
  2. Find the rows by token badge (rows are ordered by token).
  3. Tap the wrong meals. Each saves on its own; several taps in a row save in parallel.
  4. Check the Total ON footer to confirm the day's count is what you expect.
Result. Fixed in seconds, with no Save button and no page reload. If a tap is refused — "That meal can't be changed from your account" — the meal belongs to another month or your period has just ended.

Scenario 5 — "I have paid, but it still says I owe money"

Situation. Romith deposited ৳6,500 over three payments, yet his panel shows ৳368.13 due.

How to read it.

meal count 74.25 × meal rate ৳92.50 = total bill ৳6,868.13
you have paid                                   = ৳6,500.00
due                                           = ৳  368.13

Paying is not the same as paying enough. Check the three deposits listed under My deposit — if one is missing, the manager has not recorded it yet.

Result. Hand ৳368.13 to the mess manager. They record it as a deposit, and the row turns to Fully settled on both screens.

Scenario 7 — "Month end: review and close the bill"

Situation. It is 5 October. September's team must settle up.

Steps.

  1. Spend → Sum Spending → ৳59,900 spent over 15 entries.
  2. Dashboard → month meal count 647.
  3. ৳59,900 ÷ 647 = ৳92.58 → test 92.5 on Expense Status and check the table.
  4. Publish meal rate → confirm. Employees see their bills immediately.
  5. Work down Amount to be Paid: collect from the red rows, refund the green ones, recording each as a deposit.
  6. Guest tab → invoice the office ৳64,200.
  7. Report → Export CSV → file mess-report-September2026.csv.
Result. The month is closed. Every figure stays available to the September team forever; only employees' meal ON/OFF stops being editable after 5 October, 11:59 PM.
