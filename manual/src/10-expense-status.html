9Expense Status — Deposits, Meal Rate, Dues

Expense Status is where the money lives: the cash employees hand in, the rate the mess settles on, and the resulting due or refund for every person. It is one page with four stacked parts.

9.2Recording a deposit

  1. Pick the employee.
  2. Type the amount they handed over.
  3. Set the date they handed it over (today by default).
  4. Add a note if it helps — the employee will read it.
  5. Press Add deposit. The table above and the history below both update immediately.
Any number of deposits, any time

An employee can pay in one lump or in five instalments; the system simply adds them up. A deposit can be recorded before the month starts (an advance) or after it ends (a late settlement) — what matters is the month you are signed in as, not the date on the deposit.

9.3The meal rate: Test, then Publish

The rate is the single number that turns meal counts into money. It has two independent values.

9.5Where the meal rate comes from

The application does not choose the rate for you — it gives you the two numbers you need:

Spend tab       → TOTAL MONTHLY SPENDING  = ৳59,900
Dashboard       → Month meal count         = 647
------------------------------------------------
meal rate ≈ ৳59,900 ÷ 647 = ৳92.58  → published as ৳92.50
check         : 647 × ৳92.50 = ৳59,847.53 collected against ৳59,900 spent

Rounding the rate down slightly leaves the mess a few taka short; rounding up leaves a small surplus. Either is normal — the team decides. Guest meals are billed to the office separately and are not part of this arithmetic.
