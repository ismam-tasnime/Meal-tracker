8Meal Data & the Monthly Meal Table

Every meal in the system is one row: this employee, this date, breakfast yes/no, lunch yes/no, dinner yes/no. Everything else — plate counts, meal counts, bills, reports, the CSV — is calculated from those rows when it is displayed. Nothing is stored twice, so nothing can go stale.

8.1The daily meal sheet

Part of the tableWhat it shows

8.2Meal counts (weights) — how a date is priced

Weights are stored once per date, not per employee. That is what makes a correction instant and consistent.

default weights  : breakfast 0.75 · lunch 1.25 · dinner 1.00
allowed range    : 0 to 10, two decimal places
where stored     : one row per customised date, belonging to one manager period
no row           : the date uses the defaults

8.3Handover days — the 5th of the month

On the 5th, two managers are in charge of the same date. The outgoing month owns breakfast; the incoming month owns lunch and dinner. The application makes this visible rather than silently ignoring it.

MealBelongs to
4 Sep dinner, 5 Sep breakfastAugust's manager
5 Sep lunch, 5 Sep dinner, 6 Sep … 4 Oct, 5 Oct breakfastSeptember's manager
5 Oct lunch, 5 Oct dinner, 6 Oct breakfast …October's manager

Every meal belongs to exactly one month — there is no gap and no overlap, and the boundary follows real calendar dates, so February, leap years and December→January need no special handling.

8.4Making a correction

An employee who missed a deadline comes to you. The fix takes four taps:

  1. Open Meal Status.
  2. Navigate to the date in question.
  3. Find the employee's row (rows are ordered by token).
  4. Tap the meal. It saves instantly — the row's meal count and the footer totals update as you watch.
Rule — what a manager may correct
SituationAllowed?
A meal of your own period, your period is runningYes, at any time of day — deadlines do not apply to managers.
A meal of your own period, your period has endedNo. Everything is 🔒 and the page becomes a read-only record.
A meal of your own period, your period has not startedNo — the current month's manager handles it until your first day.
A meal belonging to another month (e.g. the other half of the 5th)No. Shown 🔒 and labelled Not yours.
A date outside your mess month entirelyNo. The sheet is not even loaded (Figure 8.8).

These are not screen-level restrictions. The database re-checks every single meal a write touches and rejects the whole write if any part of it is not yours — so a modified request, a copied URL or a direct API call gets exactly the same answer.

8.5How a whole month is added up

Worked through with real numbers from this manual's dataset (Romith Ahmed, token 101, September 2026):

22 breakfasts : 21 × 0.75 + 1 × 1.00 (21 Sep) = 16.75
25 lunches     : 24 × 1.25 + 1 × 1.50 (21 Sep) = 31.50
25 dinners     : 24 × 1.00 + 1 × 2.00 (16 Sep) = 26.00

Notice the two customised dates pulling their weight: 21 September (a festival lunch, breakfast 1.00 / lunch 1.50) and 16 September (biryani night, dinner 2.00). Every other date used the defaults. The answer, 74.25, is the one shown everywhere — the employee's bill, the manager's Expense Status, the Report and the CSV.
