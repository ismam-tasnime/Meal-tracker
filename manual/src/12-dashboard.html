11Dashboard Analytics

The Dashboard is the manager's one-screen answer to "where is this month?". It is a status card plus nine figures. There are no charts in this application — every figure is a single number with a label and a one-line hint telling you which period it covers.

11.1The Dashboard, card by card

#CardWhere the number comes fromPeriod it covers
1Manager period cardYour account's own period, read from the database.Fixed: 5 Sep lunch → 5 Oct breakfast.
2Status badgeWhether today falls inside the period.Right now.
3Active employeesHow many employees are marked active on the shared list.Right now — not tied to any month.
4Today's breakfast / lunch / dinnerHow many employees have that meal ON today.Today only. Shows — when the meal belongs to the neighbouring month.
5Month meal countEvery employee's weighted meal count added up.The whole period, including meals already booked for days still to come.
6Deposits receivedEvery deposit recorded in this month.The whole period.

9Still dueThe dues only, added up (refunds are not netted off).The whole period. — until a rate is set.
10Guest bill to collectThe Guest tab's total, recalculated from the guest counts.The whole period. Owed by the office, not by employees.

11.4Do the numbers update by themselves?

CardRefresh behaviour
Today's breakfast / lunch / dinnerRecalculated every time you open or reload the Dashboard. They do not auto-refresh while you watch — only the public meal board does that (every 60 seconds).
Month meal count, Deposits, Total bill, Still due, Guest billRecalculated from the underlying records on every page load. They are never cached, so they cannot be stale.
Meal rateChanges the instant you press Test or Publish.

11.5The Dashboard in the other two period states

Completed — August 2026
Figure 11.3 — A finished month keeps every figure. Only the badge and the sentence under it have changed.
Not started — October 2026
Figure 11.4 — A month that has not begun, yet already shows a meal count of 6.75 and ৳6,500 of advance deposits.
What is different?
  • August (completed) — all nine figures are still populated, because the month happened. "Today's breakfast/lunch/dinner" are absent entirely: today is not inside that period, so there is no "today" to report.
  • October (not started) — the same three cards are absent for the same reason. Total bill and Still due are dashes because no rate has been set, and Guest bill is ৳0. But two figures are already alive: Deposits received ৳6,500 (two employees paid in advance) and Month meal count 6.75 — three employees have already switched on 5 October's lunch and dinner, which are October's meals: 3 × (1.25 + 1.00) = 6.75. A month starts filling up before its first day.

With data
Figure 11.6 — The same Dashboard at the end of a full month.
What is different?
  • The layout is identical — no card disappears, and no "nothing here yet" message is shown.
  • Counts read 0; money reads ৳0; Meal rate, Total bill and Still due read —.
  • The Meal rate hint reads Not set yet rather than Published to employees.
  • The period card and its Active Manager Period badge look exactly the same, because the period exists from the moment the account is created.

A zero here means "nothing has been recorded", never "something failed". If a figure genuinely cannot be loaded, the Dashboard says so in a red box with a Try again button instead of showing a zero.
