About the screenshots in this manual

Every screenshot is a real capture of the application running against a complete month of test data (14 employees, 629 daily meal records, 32 deposits, 9 guest dates, 28 spending entries, 4 mess-month manager accounts). Nothing has been drawn, mocked or retouched. All figures — meal counts, bills, deposits, guest bills, totals — are the numbers the application itself produced.
