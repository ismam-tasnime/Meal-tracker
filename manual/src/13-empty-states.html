12Empty States

An empty screen in this application always means "nothing has been recorded yet" — never "something broke". Failures look completely different: a red box with a Try again button. This section shows every empty state so nobody ever has to guess.

You see…It means
Dashed box with a grey sentenceNo records of this kind exist yet. Normal. The sentence usually tells you what to do next.
0 or ৳0The records exist but add up to nothing.
— (a dash)The number cannot be worked out yet — almost always because no meal rate has been set.
Amber stripA rule is stopping you: outside your mess month, period not started, deadline passed.
Red box + Try againA genuine failure (usually the connection). Not an empty state. Your saved data is safe.

12.2No meals recorded

There is no separate "no meals" screen. A date on which nobody has chosen anything simply shows every button OFF, and the Total ON row reads 0 / 0 / 0 with a meal count of 0. On the employee's side the same thing shows as three OFF buttons (Figures 4.5 and 4.6). This is deliberate: "no record" and "not eating" are the same statement.

Why the table is not empty

The bill table always lists every active employee, even with no data. That is not a bug: the list of people is a fact about the office, while their meals and money are facts about the month. Seeing the names with zeros is how you confirm the month is set up correctly.

12.9What a real failure looks like

Empty states are calm and grey. Failures are red and offer a retry.

MessageWhen
Could not load dashboard stats. / Could not load meal records. / Could not load expense status. / Could not load the report. / Could not load guest meals. / Could not load spending. / Could not load employees.One part of a page could not be fetched. Shown in a red strip with Try again. The rest of the page still works.
Something went wrong while loading this page — usually a dropped connection. Your saved data is safe.The whole page failed. A Try again button reloads it in place.
Can't load your mess month / Can't load your accountThe session loaded but the month or employee behind it could not be read. Retry, then sign out and in.
Couldn't save a meal (outlined in red). Check your connection and tap it again.A meal toggle failed. The value was rolled back — tap it again.
Couldn't load today's guests, so these counts are employees only.On the meal board: the employee counts are right, the guest counts are missing. Ask the manager whether guests are expected.
