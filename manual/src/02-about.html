1About the Application

Meal Tracker is a mobile-first web application that runs an office mess: who eats which meal on which day, what the mess spends, what each person owes at the end of the month, and how many plates the cook has to prepare today.

1.1Why it exists

Before an app, an office mess runs on a paper register. Three problems repeat every month:

  • Nobody knows today's plate count until it is too late. The cook guesses, and food is wasted or runs short.
  • The register is edited after the fact. Somebody "remembers" they skipped dinner three days ago and the totals stop being trustworthy.
  • Month-end billing is a night of arithmetic. Meals × rate, minus deposits, per person, by hand.

Meal Tracker fixes all three: employees mark their own meals in advance, meals lock themselves at a published deadline, and every bill is calculated by the database from the records that already exist — so the numbers can never go stale.

A "month" here is not a calendar month. It starts with the 5th's lunch and ends with the next 5th's breakfast — for example 5 September lunch → 5 October breakfast. Each mess month has exactly one manager account, named after the month (September2026), shared by that month's team of roughly five people. On the 5th, two managers are in charge at once, each for their own meals of that day.

1.4How the money works

Four formulas run the whole application. Everything else on screen is one of these four applied to a different slice of data.

daily meal count   = breakfast ON × breakfast count + lunch ON × lunch count + dinner ON × dinner count
monthly meal count = sum of the daily meal counts over the manager period's own meals
total bill         = monthly meal count × meal rate
balance            = total deposit − total bill   (> 0 refund · = 0 settled · < 0 due)

A meal count is not the same as a meal. Breakfast is smaller than lunch, so each meal carries a weight. The default weights are Breakfast 0.75 · Lunch 1.25 · Dinner 1.00, and the mess manager can change them for any single date (a biryani night can be worth 2.00). Weights are stored once per date, not per person, so changing a date's lunch count instantly re-prices everyone who had lunch that day.

Guests and mess spending are kept completely separate from employee bills:

guest bill (one date) = breakfast guests × ৳60 + lunch guests × ৳150 + dinner guests × ৳150
total bill to collect = the same, added up over every date of the mess month (paid by the office)
total monthly spending = the sum of every spending entry recorded in the mess month

GuaranteeHow it is enforced
Nobody edits anyone else's mealsAn employee's login can write only their own meal row. Enforced by the database (Row Level Security), not just hidden in the interface.
Past meals never changeA previous day is refused outright; today's meal is refused once its deadline passes. Enforced by a database trigger, so it also blocks a hand-made API request.
Each month sees only its own dataSeptember2026 cannot see August's meal counts, deposits, rate, guests, spending or report — and vice versa.
