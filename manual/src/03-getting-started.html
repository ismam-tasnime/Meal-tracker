2.1How to reach the application

Meal Tracker is a website — there is nothing to install. Open the office link in any phone or desktop browser. Three addresses matter:

2.2Employee sign-up (Token Number)

Every employee signs up once, using the Token Number printed on the office employee list. No phone number, no e-mail, no verification message.

  1. The mess manager adds you to the employee list with your Token Number (Section 7).
  2. You open /employee/signup.
  3. Type your Token Number and choose a password of at least 6 characters, twice.
  4. Press Create account — you are signed straight in. There is no confirmation step.

Rule — one account per Token Number

A Token Number can be claimed only once, and only if it is on the employee list and the employee is active. Token numbers are not secret (they are printed on the meal board), so whoever signs up first claims it. If the wrong person claims a token, the mess manager presses Reset login (Section 7.4) and the real employee signs up again.

What you see immediately after signing in

You land on your own Employee Panel — your name, your token, today's meals, and your month. You can see nobody else's meals, deposits or bill. Section 3 walks through every part of this screen.

2.4Mess manager sign-up (one account per month)

Each month's team signs up once at /admin/signup with the month's name and a password of at least 8 characters, then shares both with the team. Signup is open — anyone with the link can claim a month nobody has taken yet — but the account can only ever manage the month written in its own name.

Rule — each month can be claimed only once

If the month already has an account, sign-up is refused and you are told to sign in instead (Figure 2.11). There is no "forgot password" for a month account either: to reset it, an administrator changes the password for that month's user in the Supabase dashboard. Nothing is deleted by a password change.

2.6Signing out

  • Employee: the Sign out button in the white card at the top of the panel. Signing out also erases the private "dummy meal rate" saved in that browser (Section 3.6).
  • Mess manager: Sign out at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer, or the last item of the scrolling tab row on a phone. It returns you to /admin/login.

2.8First-time setup (administrator, once per installation)

  1. Each month's team signs up at /admin/signup with the month name.
  2. The manager adds every employee with their Token Number under Employees.
  3. Each employee signs up once at /employee/signup.
  4. The manager checks the meal deadlines under Meal Status → 🔒 Employee meal deadlines (they start at Breakfast 8:00 AM · Lunch 11:00 AM · Dinner 5:00 PM).

That is the whole setup. There is nothing else to configure inside the application.
