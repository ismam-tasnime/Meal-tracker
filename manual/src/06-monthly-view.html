5Monthly View — Dates, Months and Totals

There is no calendar grid in this application

Meal Tracker deliberately has no month-grid calendar. Navigation is done with two separate controls that do two different jobs: a day navigator for the meal buttons, and a month navigator for your record and your money. Learning which is which takes about ten seconds and removes most of the confusion new users have.

5.1The day navigator — which day's meals am I editing?

It sits directly under the "My meals" heading and controls only the three meal buttons.

ControlWhat it does
← Prev / Next →One day back or forward.

Date boxOpens the device's date picker — the fastest way to jump a long way.

The same day navigator is used on the mess manager's Meal Status and Guest tabs, where it selects which date's sheet you are editing.

5.2The month navigator — which month's record am I reading?

Rule — a mess month is not a calendar month

September 2026 means 5 September lunch through 5 October breakfast. So 1–4 October belongs to September's record, and 1–4 September belongs to August's. When a date near the 5th looks "missing", you are almost always looking at the neighbouring mess month.

5.3How to read your month, top to bottom

  1. Check the month name first. Everything below it belongs to that month and nothing else.
  2. Read the three counters — breakfasts, lunches, dinners you have already had.
  3. Tap a counter if a number looks wrong: you get the exact dates, so you can see which day is in dispute.
  4. Read "Total meal count" — the weighted figure your bill is built from (19 × 0.75 + 23 × 1.25 + 23 × 1.00 = 67.5 in Figure 5.2).
  5. Read "My deposit" — every payment the manager has recorded for this month.
  6. Read "My bill" — the real numbers, once published.

5.5Why the month total can differ from your bill

NumberCountsWhere it appears
Total meal count (Meals I had)Past meals onlyEmployee Panel — the counters card, and the bill calculator
Meal count (bill)Every meal ON in the month, including meals booked for days still to comeEmployee Panel — My bill; Manager — Expense Status, Report, Dashboard

Late in a month the two converge; mid-month they differ by whatever you have booked ahead. Both are correct — they answer different questions ("what have I eaten?" and "what am I on the hook for?").
