18Quick Start Guide

Two pages. Print them, pin them by the mess counter.

👤 Employee — 30-second guide

  1. Sign in at /employee with your Token Number and password.
  2. Open the day you care about — Next → for tomorrow, or the date box for any day.
  3. Tap Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner to switch each ON or OFF. It saves itself; there is no Save button.
  4. Watch the padlocks. 🔒 means that meal can no longer be changed by you.
  5. Respect the deadlines — Breakfast 8:00 AM, Lunch 11:00 AM, Dinner 5:00 PM (today only; future days are always open).
  6. Scroll down for the month: meals you have had, your deposits, and your bill once it is published.

🗂 Mess Manager — 30-second guide

  1. Sign in at /admin as your month, e.g. September2026.
  2. Dashboard — check the badge is Active and read today's three plate counts.
  3. Meal Status — announce the dish, fix any meal an employee asks about, adjust a date's meal count for a special meal.
  4. Guest — type today's guest numbers the moment you hear about them.
  5. Spend — record every bazar payment the same day.
  6. Expense Status — record deposits as cash arrives.
  7. Month end — Sum Spending ÷ month meal count → Test the rate → Publish it → collect, refund, Export CSV.

The three habits that make this work

Employees: set your meals a week ahead, not the morning of. Managers: record spending and deposits on the day, never in a month-end rush. Everyone: when something looks wrong, quote the date and the meal — that is all anyone needs to fix it.
