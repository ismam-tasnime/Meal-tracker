3The Employee Panel

The Employee Panel (/employee) is a single scrolling page. Everything an employee can do in the whole application is on it. There are no sub-pages and no menus to learn.

3.1The page at a glance

2Your nameExactly as the mess manager typed it on the employee list. You cannot change it yourself.

Everything saves by itself

Tapping a meal writes it to the database immediately. A brief … means the save is in flight; a red outline means it failed and you should tap again. There is no Save button and no "unsaved changes" state to worry about.

3.3Locked meals

A padlock on the button means the meal can no longer be changed from the Employee Panel. The button still shows the truth (🔒 ON or 🔒 OFF) — it just cannot be pressed.

All three locked
Figure 3.4 — Today at 10 PM: all three deadlines have passed. The amber banner names every locked meal.
Partly locked
Figure 3.5 — Breakfast and lunch are locked, dinner is still open. The grey line underneath reads "Change before: Dinner 11:00 PM".
What is different? In Figure 3.5 the mess manager had moved the dinner deadline to 11:00 PM, so at 10:30 PM dinner was still open: its button is tappable and the banner lists only Breakfast, Lunch locked. The extra grey line "Change before: …" appears only while at least one of today's meals is still open.
Rule — three lock states, and only three
DateBreakfast / Lunch / Dinner
Before today🔒 Never editable, for any reason.
Today✅ Editable until that meal's deadline · 🔒 from the deadline onwards.
After today✅ Always editable (the deadline only applies once that day becomes today).

The lock clock re-checks itself every 15 seconds and whenever you return to the tab, so a meal locks on screen the moment its deadline passes — no reload needed. That clock follows the server's time, so a phone set to the wrong time still shows the right locks.

"Past meals only" — why this number is smaller than your bill

Meals I had counts a meal only once it can no longer be changed (its day is over, or today's deadline has passed). Meals you have already switched ON for the days ahead do not count yet. Your My bill card and the mess manager's report count every meal that is ON in the month, including the upcoming ones. In Figure 3.1 that is the difference between 67.5 (past) and 74.25 (the whole month, used for the bill).

The indigo strip inside the card restates the weights in force — Breakfast 0.75 · Lunch 1.25 · Dinner 1 — and warns that the manager may raise a day's meal count for a special meal, in which case your total rises automatically.
