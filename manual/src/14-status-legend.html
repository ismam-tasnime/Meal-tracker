13Status, Colour & Icon Guide

The application uses a small, consistent visual vocabulary. Learn these twenty signs and every screen becomes readable at a glance.

13.1Colours and what they always mean

ColourUsed forNever used for
IndigoON, the active choice, primary buttons, the Employee Panel.Warnings.
EmeraldMoney coming back (refunds), published things, confirmations, the Mess Manager Panel, ticks on the meal board.Errors.
Slate / greyOFF, inactive, settled, historical, "nothing here yet".Anything urgent.
AmberA rule is stopping you: deadlines passed, date outside your month, period not started. Also guest counts on the cook's board and the "what's cooking" card.Failures.
RedMoney owed, destructive buttons (Remove, Delete), and genuine failures.Ordinary information.
Sky blueHandover-day explanations on the 5th.Anything else.
