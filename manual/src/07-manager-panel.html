6.2The manager period — the one concept to understand first

Your account owns a manager period: a run of meals from the 5th's lunch to the next 5th's breakfast. The period decides three things and nothing else:

  1. Which meals count towards your month's totals and bills.
  2. Which employees' meals you may switch ON/OFF — and only while the period is running.
  3. Which dates you may set meal counts, guests and spending for.

Its state is shown as a badge on the Dashboard and repeated as a notice on Meal Status.

BadgeMeansCan you change employees' meals?

Manager Period Not StartedYour month has not begun yet.No — but meal counts, guests, deposits and spending for your month are already open.
Manager Period CompletedYour month is over.No — everything else about your month stays open to you forever.

A finished month loses exactly one power

When a period ends, nothing is locked away and nothing is deleted. The outgoing manager keeps their meal records (read-only), meal counts, deposits, dues, meal rate (test and publish), guests, spending, report, CSV export and every calculation — forever. The only thing that stops is switching employees' meals ON and OFF.

6.5Spend — what the mess actually cost

The Spend tab is the mess's cash book for the month: bazar runs, gas, extras. It never touches an employee's bill — it is the evidence behind the meal rate you choose.

What is different? The heading (Add spending → Edit spending), the pre-filled values, and the extra Delete button. Deleting asks for confirmation and names the entry in full ("Delete ৳4,500 spent by Rahim Mia on 5 September 2026?"). Escape, Cancel or a tap outside closes the dialog without saving.
Rules for spending entries
  • The date must be inside your mess month. Both 5ths count, so a payment made on the 5th can be recorded by either month — but never by both.
  • You can change an entry's date, name and amount, but you can never move it to another month.
  • There are no blank rows: a date with no spending simply does not appear.
  • Employees never see spending. It exists only on this tab.

6.6Report — the month-end table

The Report tab is the Expense Status table in its final, printable form: one row per employee, with the breakfast/lunch/dinner breakdown added and an Export CSV button.

ColumnWhat it contains

Export CSV

Export CSV downloads the full (unfiltered) table as mess-report-September2026.csv — one row per employee, eleven columns, ready to open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
