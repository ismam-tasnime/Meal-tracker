15The Monthly Workflow

One mess month from beginning to end, in the order things actually happen. The example throughout is September 2026: 5 September lunch → 5 October breakfast.

BEFORE THE MONTH STARTS — any time before the 5th
  1. The team signs up once at /admin/signup as September2026 and shares the password (Section 2.4).
  2. Check the employee list. Add joiners with their Token Numbers, deactivate leavers, reset any lost logins (Section 7).
  3. Chase sign-ups. Anyone still marked Not signed up cannot set their own meals.
  4. Collect advance deposits if that is your office's habit — Expense Status accepts them before the month begins (Figure 9.10).
  5. Confirm the deadlines. Meal Status → 🔒 Employee meal deadlines.

Your Dashboard reads Manager Period Not Started throughout. That is expected: everything except employees' meal ON/OFF is already yours.

Every day (mess manager, 2 minutes):

  1. Announce the dish on Meal Status → 🍲 What's cooking this date.
  2. Glance at the Dashboard — today's breakfast/lunch/dinner tell the cook what to expect.
  3. Fix any corrections employees ask for, on the date in question (Section 8.4).
  4. Declare guests the moment you hear about them — the cook's board picks them up within a minute (Section 10.3).
  5. Record the bazar on the Spend tab, same day, with the name of whoever paid.
  6. Record deposits as cash comes in, with a note (Cash / bKash / …).

Occasionally: raise a date's meal count for a special meal — a biryani night at dinner 2.00 (Section 8.2). Do it on the day, not at month end, so nobody is surprised.

END OF THE MONTH — from 5 October
  1. Close the meal data. Walk the last few days on Meal Status and settle any outstanding corrections. After 5 October, 11:59 PM this becomes impossible.
  2. Total the spending. Spend → Sum Spending → TOTAL MONTHLY SPENDING ৳59,900.
  3. Read the month meal count. Dashboard → 647.
  4. Work out the rate. ৳59,900 ÷ 647 = ৳92.58 → round to ৳92.50.
  5. Test it. Expense Status → type 92.5 → Test meal rate. Nobody else sees anything yet.
  6. Check the table. Total bill ৳59,847.53 against ৳59,900 spent — close enough. Try ৳93 or ৳95 if you want the mess to end in surplus (Figure 9.7).
  7. Publish it. Publish meal rate → confirm. Every employee's My bill fills in within seconds with their rate, bill, deposit and due or refund.
  8. Collect and refund. Work down the Amount to be Paid column; record each payment as a deposit, and watch the red rows turn to Fully settled.
  9. Invoice the office for the guest bill — ৳64,200 (Guest tab, Figure 10.7).
  10. Export the CSV from Report and file it. That is the month's permanent record.
  11. Hand over. On the 5th the incoming team takes the lunch and dinner; you keep the breakfast, and you keep everything else about your month forever.

15.1The handover day, hour by hour

5 OctoberWhose mealWho may change it
BreakfastSeptemberThe September manager (their last act).
LunchOctoberThe October manager (their first act).
DinnerOctoberThe October manager.

Both managers are "active" all day, each for their own meals only. Each month also keeps its own meal-count row and its own guest row for the 5th, so the day is split cleanly down the middle with no double-counting. A spending entry dated the 5th may be recorded by either month — but only once.

15.2What survives into the next month

ThingCarried over?
Employee list, tokens, loginsYes — it is office-wide and never resets.
Meal deadlinesYes — one office-wide setting.
Meal counts / weightsNo — each month sets its own, and unset dates use the defaults.
Meal rateNo — every month calculates its own from its own spending.
Deposits, dues, refundsNo — each month's money is its own. A refund can be re-recorded as an advance deposit in the new month.
Guests, spending, reportsNo — strictly per month.
Employees' meal choicesNo — nothing is copied forward. Everyone starts the new month with every meal OFF until they choose.
The one deadline a manager must not miss

Employee meal ON/OFF becomes uneditable at 11:59 PM on the period's last day — 5 October for September's team. Every other task (rate, collection, refunds, reports, CSV) can be done at leisure afterwards. Settle the meal data first, then take your time over the money.
